# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project.

Be constructive, assume good intent, respect differing viewpoints and experiences, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer at `austinbrown101@msn.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.
